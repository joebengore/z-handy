## 8. Three-Year Limited Workmanship Warranty

**Coverage.** Contractor warrants that the Work will be free from defects in workmanship for
a period of **three (3) years** from the date of Substantial Completion (the "Warranty
Period"). Covered defects are limited to peeling, blistering, flaking, chipping, or
delamination of the applied coating that is attributable to Contractor's surface preparation
or application, and to excessive or uneven fading or discoloration attributable to
Contractor's application.

**Sole and exclusive remedy.** Contractor's sole obligation, and Client's sole and exclusive
remedy, for a covered defect is, at Contractor's option, to **repair or repaint the affected
area** using the same or comparable materials, at no charge for labor or materials.
Contractor does not warrant an exact color match between original and touched-up or repainted
areas, as coatings weather and manufacturers' formulations change. Contractor may elect to
coat to the nearest natural break line rather than repaint an entire surface or elevation.

**Exclusions.** This warranty does **not** cover, and Contractor is not responsible for:

(a) defects in the coating product itself (which are covered, if at all, by the
    manufacturer's written warranty, which Contractor hereby assigns to Client to the extent
    assignable);
(b) failures caused by moisture intrusion, water leaks, high humidity, condensation,
    hydrostatic pressure, efflorescence, or improper drainage, ventilation, flashing, caulk,
    grade, or roofing;
(c) mold, mildew, algae, or fungal growth;
(d) rust, corrosion, wood rot, insect or pest damage, or deterioration of the underlying
    substrate;
(e) settling, structural movement, cracking of stucco, drywall, masonry, or joints, or
    nail pops;
(f) failure or incompatibility of previously applied coatings, or of substrates that Client
    declined to have prepared, primed, repaired, or replaced as recommended by Contractor
    (see Section 9);
(g) surfaces not painted by Contractor, materials supplied by Client or others, and areas
    outside the Scope of Work;
(h) damage caused by Client, Client's tenants, occupants, pets, agents, or other
    contractors; vandalism; abrasion; pressure washing; harsh cleaners or solvents;
    impact; or abuse or misuse;
(i) hurricanes, tropical storms, named windstorms, floods, hail, lightning, fire, freeze,
    earthquake, or other acts of God or casualty;
(j) normal wear and tear, ordinary fading, chalking, or weathering consistent with the
    product's expected performance in a coastal or subtropical climate;
(k) horizontal or walked-on surfaces, decks, floors, stairs, driveways, roofs, and pool
    decks unless expressly listed in Section 1 as warranted;
(l) galvanized metal, aluminum, vinyl, plastic, cedar, redwood, pressure-treated lumber,
    and other bleed-through- or adhesion-prone substrates, unless expressly warranted in
    writing; and
(m) any Work for which Contractor has not been paid in full.

**Conditions.** This warranty is conditioned on (i) payment of the Contract Price in full,
(ii) Client's **written notice** to Contractor describing the defect and its location,
delivered within **thirty (30) days** after the defect is discovered and in all events before
