Any additional or corrective work requires an approved written change order. If the parties do not agree, Contractor may leave the affected work undone or end the Agreement; Customer will pay for work already completed and materials or costs reasonably committed for the job.

Dates are estimates and may change due to weather, material availability, unsafe conditions, Customer decisions or access, or events beyond Contractor's reasonable control. Contractor will notify Customer of material delays.
